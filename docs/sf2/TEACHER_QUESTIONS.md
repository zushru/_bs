# SF2 questions for the adviser/teacher

These questions remain unanswered. The current implementation records the user's provisional/local convention but does not claim official acceptance.

1. **AM/PM markings:** Is the following local legend accepted for the SF2 your school submits? Blank = attended both sessions; upper-left shaded = morning only; lower-right shaded = afternoon only; `E` = excused; `x` = absent both sessions.
2. **Excused combinations:** The device will count `E` as Present in monthly totals. The existing builder prints `E` if a learner is excused in one session and absent in the other, but prints blank if the other session was attended. Is that exact daily-cell behaviour what your adviser uses?
3. **One-session class day:** If AM is declared no-class but PM is scheduled (or vice versa), what exact cell should be shown when the learner attends or misses the one scheduled session?
4. **Late/Tardy:** Should Late remain an attendance mark only, or should it affect the daily cell, monthly TARDY total, or both? How is the TARDY total computed?
5. **Semester field:** For the school year being reported, what exact value should be written in the template's `Semester` field when the calendar is organized by terms?
6. **Incomplete coverage:** If the device was off or records are missing, what school-approved process is used to resolve the date before it is reported? Who may authorize the correction and what evidence is retained?
7. **Historical start:** If the device began recording in the middle of a month/term, how are earlier attendance records supplied or identified as unavailable?
8. **Template acceptance:** Is this exact uploaded SHSF-2 workbook and the customized AM/PM legend accepted by the school/LIS for the intended Regular SHS and Strengthened SHS cohorts?

Confirmed from the user's directions: include learners whose enrollment overlaps any part of the month, including transfers out; one device is configured for one section; ask for section during setup; support either Regular SHS or Strengthened SHS as the configured curriculum profile.
